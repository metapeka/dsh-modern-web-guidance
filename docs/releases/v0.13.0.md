# 0.13.0 — the report states a direction, and more often says why it cannot 🧭

Phase F answers a question the counters could hold since 0.10.0 and never asked: whether things are moving. Every
answer carries its sample, and most of them are refusals — four tokens for a project's slope, four for a rule's,
four for the corpus answer rate — because a direction needs a sample, one tree and a denominator.

## ✨ Features

- **A project slope: `Slope: 6 scans: 12.4 → 9.1 findings per 100 files`.** The newest comparable half of the ring
  against the half before it, in rates rather than counts, with the sample in the sentence: a rate over four scans
  and one over twenty are different claims — and the panel draws it in the comparison's style, never as a recovery.
- **A rule's own direction, with its sample.** `Rule slopes: ls-17 rising: 9 → 15 over 4 scans, ls-08
  too-few-samples (2 scans), ls-05 not-sampled` — one clause per ranked rule, over the entries that carry it and
  trimmed to the same number of carrying entries on both sides, because summing whole halves can invert it.
- **The corpus answer rate, beside the hit rate.** `Guidance answered 84% of what it was asked in the last 30
  days (120 of 143), against 78% in the 30 days before (96 of 123).` It divides answered guidance calls by asked
  ones (`list` answers, `error` belongs to neither side), while the KPI keeps dividing hits by every call.
- **The suppression file is listed, not judged.** The review carries every entry — its id, the file's own date
  and reason, and `matched` in three states — and the panel draws the file itself: a row per entry, a pill per
  verdict, a Remove on every row including a live mute, one write block, and the reader's scroll kept on a write.

## 🧪 Behaviour changes

- **It refuses on a small sample.** `Slope: not comparable (too-few-scans)` under four comparable scans — two
  halves of two, because one scan per half is a difference between two points, not a direction.
- **It refuses on two branches, and on a missing denominator.** `branch-changed` needs two labelled halves that
  disagree; one labelled half states its slope and says nothing about branches. `no-files, older half` names the
  half that has no files, because "older" is not a token, it is the answer.
- **It refuses on an aggregate.** `Slope: not comparable (aggregate)` prints where a comparison stays silent: a
  slope is the per-workspace claim, so the aggregate is exactly where a reader must be told there is none.
- **A truncated scan is a hole, not a zero.** Dropped before the halves are cut, so the sample shrinks and the
  boundary moves; counting it as "found nothing" would be the most flattering lie the report could tell.
- **The ten is a sample, not a census.** The ring keeps each scan's ten most-numerous rules, so a rule absent from
  an entry was below that scan's top ten — never silent. Hence `entered-sample` and `left-sample`, which are not
  `new` and not `resolved`, and `not-sampled` when no entry ever carried the rule.
- **An erased baseline is not an improvement.** `no-previous` prints the rate it can measure and no comparison,
  so a reset can never read as a jump to 100 %; `thin` prints counts without a percentage, `too-old` refuses a
  window the ninety-day retention already pruned, and `no-days` prints nothing at all, never "0 %".

## 🛠 Hardening

- **Three states, not two, and a list that is never truncated.** No ring means no `scanSlope` member, a ring this
  host cannot read is `null` ("cannot tell"), an empty one is the refusal `too-few-scans`; above **200** suppression
  entries the panel gets the counts and no rows, because a prefix would read as the whole file.

## 🧩 Compatibility

- **`version` stays `1`, nothing new is stored, and the surface is unchanged.** Slopes and both rates are derived at
  render time (a report leaves `stats.json` byte-identical); four tools, four human commands, no new dependency, no chart.

<summary>展开</summary>

- 项目趋势：`Slope: 6 scans: 12.4 → 9.1 findings per 100 files` —— 环形历史最新可比较的一半对比之前的一半，用比率
  而不是计数，样本写在句子里；面板里同一行就放在对比行下面。
- 每条规则的趋势带自己的样本：`Rule slopes: ls-17 rising: 9 → 15 over 4 scans, ls-08 too-few-samples (2 scans)`
  —— 只统计确实带有该规则的条目，并把两半裁剪到相同条目数，否则把整半相加会把方向算反。
- 语料库的「回答率」与 KPI 的「命中率」并列：`Guidance answered 84% … (120 of 143), against 78% … (96 of 123).`
  `list` 算作回答，`error` 不属于任何一边，而 KPI 仍是命中除以所有调用。
- 屏蔽文件现在是列出来看，而不是下判断：每条记录带着自己的 id、文件里的日期与原因，`matched` 有三种状态，
  面板逐条列出，每个结论一颗药丸，每行（包括仍然生效的屏蔽）都有 Remove。
- 拒绝是正常结果：`too-few-scans`（少于四次可比较扫描）、`branch-changed`（两条分支）、`no-files, older half`
  （缺分母）以及 `aggregate` —— 后者在这里会打印，而旧的对比行是沉默的。
- 被截断的扫描是洞，不是零：在切半之前就被丢弃。十条规则是样本不是普查，所以 `left-sample` 不是 `resolved`，
  `entered-sample` 也不是 `new`。
- 被抹掉的基线不是进步：`no-previous` 只打印它能测到的比率，不做比较。打包：**258 个文件、1.0 MB、解包
  3.0 MB**，**562 个测试 / 18 个文件**（558 通过，4 跳过因 symlink），**连跑 20 次无失败**；`version` 仍为 1。

</details>
